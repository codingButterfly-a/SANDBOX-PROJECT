About Us

At RoboEat, we believe a great steak shouldn't be hard to find — or hard to afford. We serve premium steakhouse flavors with the speed and friendliness of your favourite neighbourhood spot. Whether you dine in or grab takeout, every plate leaves our kitchen with the same care and quality.

Our Story

RoboEat started with a simple idea: make the kind of fire-grilled steak dinner you'd normally reserve for special occasions available any day of the week. We source only AAA Canadian beef, age our cuts for a minimum of 50 days, and season everything in-house so every bite is bold from the first cut to the last.

Since opening our doors, we've been committed to keeping the menu focused — a handful of outstanding cuts, house-made sides, and an experience that feels consistent every single visit.
